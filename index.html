Welcome

DecisionChronicles is a narrative decision-making game set in a post-apocalyptic world, where every choice influences the future of humanity.

Three factions fight for control of tomorrow. Flip cards, make difficult choices, and face the consequences of every decision.

About

In a world on the verge of collapse, several groups struggle to impose their vision of the future. Your decisions will shape the fate of survivors and determine which path humanity will follow.

DecisionChronicles combines narrative tension, strategic choices, and lasting consequences in a dark and immersive universe.

The Game

Flip the cards to make difficult decisions. Every choice impacts the balance of the world and the fate of the different factions.

  • Story-driven decision gameplay
  • Post-apocalyptic setting
  • Competing factions with different ideologies
  • Choices with lasting consequences
  • Strategic survival and narrative tension
